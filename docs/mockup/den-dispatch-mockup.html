Dispatch cards: the agents' work, in the orchestrator chat

When the orchestrator hands a brief to an agent, the agent's own turn shows up here as a card it owns: avatar and colour, the brief, a live ticker while it works, and the real transcript when you expand it. Nothing is retold by the orchestrator. The chat survives a restart.
